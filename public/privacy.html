Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 11, 2026

Summary: We collect only what's necessary to connect workers with employers. We never sell your data. You can delete your account anytime.

1. Information We Collect

When you use DutyPe, we collect:

  • Account Information: Phone number, name, profile photo (optional)
  • Location Data: To show jobs near you (only when app is open)
  • Job Applications: Jobs you apply to and your application status
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version for app optimization

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To show relevant jobs near your location
  • To connect you with employers
  • To send job alerts and application updates
  • To improve our app and services
  • To prevent fraud and ensure safety

3. Information Sharing

We share your information only in these cases:

  • With Employers: When you apply for a job, employers see your profile
  • Service Providers: Firebase (hosting), Google (analytics, maps)
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or to protect safety

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure cloud storage (Firebase/Google Cloud)
  • Regular security audits

5. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update incorrect information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all data
  • Opt-out: Disable notifications anytime

6. Location Data

Location is collected only when the app is open. You can disable location access in your device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

DutyPe is not intended for users under 18 years of age.

For privacy-related questions: dutypein@gmail.com
