Frequently Asked Questions

👷 For Workers

Is DutyPe free for workers?

Yes! DutyPe is 100% free for job seekers. You never need to pay anything to find or apply for jobs.

How do I apply for a job?

Simply browse jobs near you, tap on a job you like, and click "Apply Now". Your profile will be shared with the employer.

Should I pay any fee to get a job?

NO! Never pay any fee to anyone for a job. If someone asks for money, report them immediately using the Report button.

How do I know if my application was seen?

Go to "My Jobs" tab to see all your applications and their status (Pending, Under Review, Accepted, etc.)

Can I apply to multiple jobs?

Yes! You can apply to as many jobs as you want. There's no limit.

How do I withdraw my application?

Go to My Jobs → find your application → tap "Withdraw". You can withdraw anytime before being hired.

🏢 For Employers

How do I post a job?

Sign up as an employer, complete your profile, and tap "Post Job" to create a new job listing.

Is posting jobs free?

Basic job posting is free. Premium features like priority listing and unlimited contact views may require a subscription.

How do I contact applicants?

View applications in your dashboard, and use the in-app chat or call feature to contact workers.

Can I edit my job after posting?

Yes, you can edit job details anytime from your job management screen.

How do I mark a position as filled?

Go to your job listing → tap "Mark as Filled". This will stop new applications.

🔒 Account & Privacy

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. All your data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

Is my data safe?

Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and secure cloud storage. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I change my phone number?

Contact support at dutypein@gmail.com to change your registered phone number.

How do I report a suspicious job?

Open the job listing → tap the 🚩 Report button → select reason → submit. We review all reports within 24 hours.

💳 Payments

How do I get a refund?

Email dutypein@gmail.com within 24 hours of purchase. See our Refund Policy for details.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and wallets through Razorpay.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Subscription → Cancel. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.
