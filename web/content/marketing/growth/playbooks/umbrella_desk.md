# Playbook — Umbrella help desk (field registration)

> Goal: one person, one umbrella, one table → **40+ people registered a day**, **25+ of them
> actually joined in the app within 7 days**. Workers, service partners, employers and customers.

## Kit (one-time ≈ ₹3,500)

- Branded umbrella + small folding table and 2 stools (₹2,000).
- A2 flex banner in Telugu: "ఉద్యోగాలు · అర్జెంట్ పని · ఇంటి సేవలు — ఇక్కడే ఉచితంగా రిజిస్టర్ చేసుకోండి" (₹400).
- 300 A5 pamphlets with the agent's QR (Marketing kit → Print poster, campaign tagged) (₹600).
- Phone with mobile data + hotspot, power bank. DutyPe T-shirt / ID card for the agent.
- Agent code from the admin panel (Admin → Field registrations → *Add agent*), e.g. `RAVI01`.
  Bookmark **dutype.in/join?agent=RAVI01** on the agent's phone.

## Where and when

| Time | Place | Who comes |
|---|---|---|
| 7:00–10:00 | Bus stand / labour adda | Daily-wage workers, helpers, masons, drivers |
| 10:30–13:30 | Wyra Road, main bazaar, hardware & electrical shops | Shop owners (employers), electricians, plumbers |
| 15:00–18:00 | Hospital, college, ITI gates | Young workers, nurses' helpers, students for part-time |
| Sunday | Weekly market / apartment complexes (with RWA permission) | Customers for home services, house help |

Ask permission from the shop owner / RWA / bus-stand in-charge. Never block the road.

## Per person (≈ 3 minutes)

1. Greet in Telugu, ask: **"పని కావాలా, మనిషి కావాలా, లేదా ఇంట్లో రిపేర్ పని ఉందా?"**
   (Need work, need a worker, or need a repair at home?)
2. Open **dutype.in/join?agent=CODE**, choose who they are:
   - **Worker** — wants a job / daily work.
   - **Service partner** — electrician, plumber, AC tech, cleaner, carpenter, painter… (tick the skills).
   - **Employer** — shop / office / home that needs staff.
   - **Customer** — wants a home service.
3. Name, mobile number, area, skills. **Read the consent line aloud** and tick it only if they say yes.
4. Help them **install DutyPe and log in with the same number** (WhatsApp OTP / Truecaller).
   This is what pays the agent — the lead turns *JOINED* automatically.
5. Partners: tell them the rules in one breath — "ఫిక్స్‌డ్ ధర, కస్టమర్ నేరుగా మీకే చెల్లిస్తారు,
   ఒక్కో పనికి DutyPeకి ₹19 మాత్రమే, మొదటి పని ఉచితం. ఎలక్ట్రికల్ / AC / ప్లంబింగ్‌కి మా టీమ్
   ఫోన్‌లో స్కిల్ చెక్ చేస్తుంది."
6. Give a pamphlet. Move on — don't spend 15 minutes with one person.

## Never

- Never take money from anyone (registration is free). Never keep anyone's Aadhaar / phone.
- Never fill a form for someone who did not agree, or with a made-up number — duplicates and fakes
  are visible in the panel and are not paid.
- Never promise a job or income. Say "jobs near you come in the app".

## Agent pay (suggested)

- Fixed ₹12,000–15,000 a month **plus ₹20 per person who joins in the app** (shown as *Joined* in
  the admin panel), **plus ₹50 per service partner who completes 3 jobs**.
- Weekly target: 150 joined. Review every Friday with the panel numbers.

## Team follow-up (same day)

- Admin → Field registrations → filter *NEW*: call partners within 24 h for the skill check,
  employers within 24 h to post their first job (offer to post it for them).
- Mark *CALLED* / *NOT_INTERESTED*; *JOINED* is set automatically when they sign up.
