# All DutyPe strategies & plans — master list (Khammam, 2026)

> One page that lists everything we decided, so nothing is forgotten. Details live in the linked
> pages: **khammam_launch_2026** (launch plan), **growth/playbooks/umbrella_desk** (help desk SOP),
> and the admin **Posters & Marketing Kit** / **Field Registrations** / **DutyPe Services** pages.
> ✅ = built in the app or admin · 📋 = a plan for the team to run.

## 1. What we sell and to whom
- ✅ **Jobs (vacancies)** — weekly or monthly pay only (₹3,000–₹40,000/month, ₹1,000–₹10,000/week; full-time ≥ ₹8,000 / ₹2,000). Daily work goes to urgent posts.
- ✅ **Urgent work** — short, same-day pay, ₹200–₹2,000 per person, offers reach nearby workers in minutes.
- ✅ **DutyPe Services** — fixed-price home services (cleaning, AC, electrician, plumber, appliances, carpenter, painting, home help, car wash) in Khammam district.
- ✅ **One app for everyone** — a worker can also book a home service; a shop owner can hire staff and book services.
- ✅ **Role screen**: "నాకు పని కావాలి" (I want work) and "నాకు మనిషి / ఇంటి సేవ కావాలి" (I need workers or home services).

## 2. Supply first
- 📋 8–10 approved partners per service category before advertising it; switch a category off in admin until then.
- 📋 Launch area first: 3–5 km of Khammam town (Wyra Road, Gandhi Chowk, Mamillagudem, bus stand, collectorate).
- 📋 Find partners at electrical / hardware / sanitary shops (₹50 to the shop per partner who finishes 3 jobs), ITI Khammam, AC dealers' technicians, mestri networks, the labour adda.
- ✅ **Basic vs skilled**: cleaning / home help / car wash = anyone careful; electrical / AC / plumbing / appliance / carpentry / painting need ≥ 1 year and a skill check before approval.
- 📋 Skill check: 3 phone questions, photos/videos of past work or a shop/ITI reference, trial job if unsure; first 5 jobs simple ones.
- 📋 Later: "helper" training (new person works with a senior partner, then gets checked); partner referral reward (₹50 credits when the referred partner finishes 3 jobs).

## 3. Prices, fees, offers (DutyPe never pays from its pocket)
- ✅ Prices set from Khammam/Hyderabad market rates (middle of the local range — not too cheap, not too costly).
- ✅ Customer booking fee ₹19 (₹49 for inspection visits), partner fee ₹19 per job (₹9 possible in admin), first partner job free.
- ✅ First customer booking: ₹0 booking fee. Employers on a paid plan: ₹0 booking fee on every service.
- ✅ Coupons / festive offers (Dasara, Diwali, Sankranti, Weekend presets): once per customer, never stacked, capped at DutyPe's own fee — the partner's earning never changes.
- ✅ Money flow: customer pays the partner after the work (cash/UPI); DutyPe's fees are taken from the partner's prepaid credits (top-up by UPI, UTR checked in admin). Credits are non-refundable and stay for future jobs.
- 📋 No collecting customers' money into our bank (RBI payment-aggregator rules). Later, if prepaid booking is needed: Razorpay Route / Cashfree split.
- ✅ Referral: each friend who joins gives an employer one free post (normal or urgent) for 24 hours.

## 4. Trust and safety
- ✅ Code of conduct screens for partners, workers and employers (Telugu/English/Hindi); partners accept before applying.
- ✅ "Keep ready" (broom, mop, ladder…) and "partner brings" (tools, tester…) shown on every service.
- ✅ Start code, ratings after every service, rate-your-last-service card, free re-work within 7 days.
- ✅ A worker on an unfinished job cannot take another one.
- ✅ Terms & privacy: misuse → account block and police complaint (BNS 2023, IT Act, child/bonded labour, wages); DPDP rights.
- ✅ Urgent posts: employer can mark filled early (e.g. a worker brings a friend) or remove a worker with a reason; DutyPe AI can do both.

## 5. Login cost (biggest running cost)
- ✅ WhatsApp code first (~₹0.12), then the same code by our SMS gateway (~₹0.2), Firebase SMS (~₹6.7) only as a last resort.
- ✅ A code works 10 minutes; "resend" sends the same code (up to 30 minutes); waits grow 30 s → 60 s → 2 min → 5 min; 5 codes/hour and 10/day per number; 5 wrong tries lock the code; 20 requests/hour per network; daily spend caps.
- ✅ Truecaller one-tap login.
- 📋 Register a DLT OTP template (2Factor or MSG91) and add the keys to the functions env.

## 6. Channels (where we find people)
- ✅📋 **Umbrella help desk** — field agent at bus stand (7–10 am), bazaar (11–2), hospital/college gates (3–6), Sunday market; registers people on dutype.in/join?agent=CODE; paid per person who actually joins.
- ✅📋 **Posters** — clean Telugu-first style (white, one colour, big headline, one offer, QR), A4 print; at electrical/hardware shops, apartments, PGs, tea stalls, bus stops.
- ✅ **Instagram** 4:5 posts and 9:16 stories from the Marketing kit (text in safe zones).
- 📋 **Local creators** — 3–5 Khammam creators (10k–50k real followers, ~₹1k–12k per reel), each with their own coupon code.
- 📋 **Auto-rickshaw back stickers** (~₹140–270/auto/month) for brand recall; newspaper inserts only for festival offers.
- ✅ **WhatsApp templates** (customers, festive, employers, workers, students, partners, referral) — only to people who agreed; one promo a week.
- 📋 **Students** — college/ITI gates 3–6 pm, hostels, coaching centres, student WhatsApp groups, one campus ambassador per college (₹20 per student who joins). Pitch: "చదువుతూనే సంపాదించండి — కాలేజ్ దగ్గర్లో పార్ట్-టైమ్ పనులు, వారం జీతం, ఏజెంట్ ఫీజు లేదు." New categories: Home Tutor, Event Staff, Store Promoter, Pharmacy Assistant, Construction; "Student friendly" perk.
- 📋 **Employers** — supermarkets, restaurants, hospitals, schools, PGs, builders; offer to post their first job for them.

## 7. 90-day plan and weekly rhythm
- 📋 Weeks 1–3 partners + workers; 4–6 employers + first customers; 7–9 festival push; 10–13 repeat bookings and referrals.
- 📋 Targets: 60 partners, 1,000 workers, 150 employers, 600 bookings by week 9, ≥ 4.5★, < 10% "no partner".
- 📋 Every Friday: bookings, no-partner rate, cancellations, low ratings, partner earnings, cost per joined user per channel — cut the worst channel, double the best.

## 8. Things we decided NOT to do (for now)
- Before/after photos on completion (optional later).
- 6-month AC service reminders (later).
- Collecting full payment into our bank account.
- Very cheap prices to win customers.
- Paying field agents per form (only per joined person).
