## 1. Executive Summary: The Khammam Flywheel

In Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian cities like Khammam, traditional marketplace models (charging 15%–20% commission on home service technicians or daily-wage workers) fail due to **worker churn and offline leakage**:
* A plumber or electrician charged a ₹60 cut on a ₹300 job immediately asks the customer for their personal phone number for all future visits.
* Workers turn off the app, distrust the platform, and supply dries up.

### Why Zero Commission for Workers Wins:
1. **Supply Monopolization**: By charging **0% commission** to technicians and workers, 90%+ of Khammam's skilled plumbers, electricians, cleaners, and helpers register, stay online, and keep DutyPe active.
2. **Zero Offline Leakage**: Workers have zero incentive to bypass DutyPe because they keep 100% of their cash and UPI earnings.
3. **High-Margin Alternative Revenue**: Instead of taking money from blue-collar labor, DutyPe monetizes **Customer Convenience**, **Employer Hiring Subscriptions**, and **Hyperlocal Business Advertising**.

## 2. Revenue Stream 1: Customer Platform / Convenience Fee

Every customer booking a home service (Cleaning, Electrician, Plumber, Carpenter, Painter, Maid, Babysitter) pays a micro platform fee directly during booking confirmation.

### Dynamic Pricing Tier
$$\text{Platform Fee} = \begin{cases} ₹9 & \text{for bookings } \le ₹149 \text{ (e.g. minor inspection, tap washer fix)} \\ ₹19 & \text{for bookings } > ₹149 \text{ (e.g. deep cleaning, rewiring, installation)} \end{cases}$$

* **Frictionless Conversion**: ₹9–₹19 is perceived as negligible (less than a cup of tea or auto fare) by homeowners, eliminating checkout drop-off.
* **Volume Math**: 300 completed bookings daily in Khammam = 9,000 monthly bookings $\times$ ₹15 avg fee = **₹1,35,000 / month** at 98%+ gross margin.
* **Standardized UPI Rail**: Standardized to `dutypein@ybl` across all payment gateways and QR displays.

## 3. Revenue Stream 2: Employer Subscriptions & Hiring Passes

Local businesses in Khammam (restaurants, sweet shops, hospitals, private schools, retail cloth showrooms, automobile workshops, and builders) experience constant employee turnover and urgently need staff.

### Subscription Plans
1. **Launch Pass (₹199 / month)**:
   * Up to 5 active job postings per month.
   * Direct calling & WhatsApp unlock for applicant contact numbers.
   * Standard 3 km geographic visibility.
2. **Business Pro (₹499 / month)**:
   * Unlimited standard job postings.
   * Priority urgent blast pings to available workers within 15 minutes.
   * Access to verified candidate database and direct WhatsApp chat.
   * "DutyPe Verified Employer" trust badge on job cards.
3. **Employer Referral Loop**:
   * Employers can earn **1 Free Job Posting** for every **2 verified business referrals** who register and post their first requirement, lowering CAC to near zero.
