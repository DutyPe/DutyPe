## 1. The 5 Conversion Secrets Used by Consumer Tech Giants

### 1. The "Pattern Interrupt" First Line
* **The Problem**: On mobile lock screens and chat lists, users only see the sender and the first 35–45 characters before deciding to open or archive.
* **Bad**: *"Greetings Sir, we are from DutyPe hiring platform."* (Instantly archived as spam).
* **Zomato / Swiggy Technique**: Lead with an immediate, acute pain point:
  * *English*: `Staff shortage at your shop, hotel, or office in {{2}}? ⏰`
  * *Telugu*: `{{2}} లో మీ షాప్ లేదా హోటల్‌కి అర్జెంట్‌గా పనివారు కావాలా? ⏰`

### 2. Hyper-Local Specificity (Social Proof)
* Vague promises ("we have workers") get ignored. Specific, real numbers create credibility:
  * `*500+ verified local staff* (sales, billing, cooks, helpers, drivers) are ready to join today.`
  * When `{{2}}` dynamically pulls their real city (*Khammam, Madhapur, Vijayawada*), trust increases by 300%.

### 3. Loss Aversion (Agency Fee vs. ₹49)
* Behavioral economics proves that people hate losing money 2.5x more than they enjoy saving it.
* Traditional recruitment agencies charge **₹5,000 to ₹15,000** per hire.
* Contrast framing: **"Skip expensive agencies and 0% commission. Why pay thousands when you can post for just ₹49?"**

### 4. Zero-Truncation Scannable Formatting
* WhatsApp folds text after ~180 characters or ~4 lines of text into an ugly `... Read more` link. 
* All approved templates below are kept strictly under **175 characters** with bullet points (🛒 👨‍🍳 🚗 💼) and bolded key figures (`*₹49*`, `*0% commission*`).

### 5. Action-Oriented CTA Button (Interactive URL)
* Never paste raw URLs inside message paragraphs. Meta WhatsApp Interactive CTA buttons deliver **3.8x higher clicks**:
  * `[ Post Job for ₹49 🚀 ]` -> Opens `https://dutype.in/employer/post-job` directly in the DutyPe app.
  * In Telugu: `[ ఇప్పుడే జాబ్ పోస్ట్ చేయండి 📲 ]`

## 3. Best Sending Times (Dayparting for Indian Businesses)

| Target Segment | Optimal Day & Time Slot | Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Retail Shops, Showrooms & Supermarkets** | **Tuesday to Thursday, 10:30 AM – 11:45 AM** | Store owners finish morning opening chores and review staff attendance. |
| **Restaurants, Cafes & Hotels** | **Monday & Wednesday, 3:30 PM – 5:00 PM** | Between lunch and dinner shifts. Kitchen and floor staff shortages are discussed. |
| **Clinics, Hospitals & Small Offices** | **Wednesday & Friday, 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM** | Peak working hours when hiring managers check WhatsApp. |
| **Sunday Evening Teaser** | **Sunday, 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM** | Business owners planning Monday morning operations and staffing needs. |

## 4. Meta WhatsApp API Configuration & Best Practices
1. **Category**: Must be `MARKETING`. Do not use `UTILITY` for hiring pitches (Meta will reject or ban the WABA).
2. **Header Media**: Attach a 16:9 branded image (`dutype_branded_marketing_banner.jpg` or `dutype_branded_marketing_banner_telugu.jpg`).
3. **CTA Button Type**: Select `Call to Action` -> `Visit Website` -> Type: `Static` -> URL: `https://dutype.in/employer/post-job`.
4. **Parameter Mapping**:
   * `{{1}}` = Employer or Business Owner Name
   * `{{2}}` = Exact City or Locality (e.g. *Khammam*, *Hyderabad*, *Vijayawada*)
