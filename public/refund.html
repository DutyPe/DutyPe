Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: January 11, 2026

🆓 For Workers: DutyPe is 100% FREE for job seekers. You never need to pay anything to find jobs.

1. Employer Subscriptions

This policy applies to employer subscription plans purchased through DutyPe.

2. Refund Eligibility

Situation Refund
Request within 24 hours of purchase (unused) ✅ Full refund
Technical issues preventing service use ✅ Full or partial refund
Duplicate payment ✅ Full refund of duplicate
Service used for more than 7 days ❌ No refund
Account terminated for policy violation ❌ No refund

3. How to Request a Refund

  1. Open DutyPe app → Settings → Contact Us
  2. Or email dutypein@gmail.com
  3. Include your registered phone number and payment details
  4. Describe the reason for refund request

4. Refund Processing

  • Refund requests are reviewed within 3-5 business days
  • Approved refunds are processed within 7-10 business days
  • Refunds are credited to the original payment method
  • Bank processing may take additional 5-7 days

5. Subscription Cancellation

  • You can cancel your subscription anytime from the app
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of current billing period
  • You retain access until the subscription expires
  • No partial refunds for unused days in current period

6. Auto-Renewal

  • Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled
  • You'll receive a reminder before renewal
  • Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid charges

7. Contact for Refunds

For refund-related queries:

  • Email: dutypein@gmail.com
  • Response time: 24-48 hours
