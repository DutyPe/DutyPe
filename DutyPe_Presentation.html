🚨 The Problem We're Solving

📍 Location Mismatch

Workers waste hours traveling to jobs far from home. A cleaner won't travel 20km for a 2-hour shift. Current platforms ignore hyperlocal needs.

🎭 Rampant Fraud

"Earn ₹50,000/day from home" scams flood job boards. Workers lose money to fake registration fees. Employers get ghosted by fake applicants.

📱 Complex Interfaces

Existing apps are designed for IT professionals. Blue-collar workers struggle with complex forms, English-only interfaces, and resume requirements.

💸 Payment Uncertainty

Workers fear not getting paid. Employers fear workers not showing up. No trust mechanism exists for daily wage jobs.

✨ Our Solution: DutyPe

📍

Hyperlocal First

Jobs sorted by distance. See "400 meters away" not just addresses. If it's not nearby, it doesn't exist.

🛡️

Zero Fraud

Structured job titles, location verification, pay rate guardrails, and community reporting. Scammers can't survive here.

🎯

Simple & Visual

No resumes needed. 1-tap apply. Voice descriptions. Designed for workers who navigate by landmarks, not addresses.

ZERO FRAUD

🛡️ Safety & Trust Features

🔒 Developer Mode Detection

Detects if Developer Options are enabled (fake GPS risk). Shows security warning and requires user to disable it before using the app.

📍 Location Verification

GPS-based location verification. Employer location compared with job location. Flags suspicious postings (>50km distance).

👤 Age Validation

Users must be 18-70 years old. Date of birth validation prevents underage or overage users from completing profile.

📱 Device Fingerprinting

Stores device fingerprint to prevent banned users from creating new accounts. Tracks Android ID, device model, and timestamps.

⭐ Two-Way Rating System

Workers rate employers, employers rate workers. Builds trust scores. Categories: Punctuality, Quality, Communication, Professionalism.

🚫 Dual-Role Prevention

Same phone number cannot be both Worker and Employer. Prevents fraud and maintains role integrity.
