# DutyPe: The Instant Workforce Network

This document outlines the vision, features, and monetization strategies for the Employer side of DutyPe, specifically focusing on the **Instant Hire** feature.

---

## 1. Vision: More Than a Job App

DutyPe is not just a job board (like Apna, WorkIndia, Naukri, or Indeed) that focuses on scheduled hiring. We are building an **"Instant Workforce Network."**

Think of it like this:
- **Uber** = Instant Ride
- **Swiggy** = Instant Food
- **DutyPe** = Instant Workers

This is a much stronger positioning.

---

## 2. Feature Name: ⚡ Instant Hire

We are moving away from "Instant Job Posting" or "Urgent Work Requests". The feature is simple, memorable, and powerful: **⚡ Instant Hire**.

### Use Cases (Real Everyday Needs)
Imagine someone suddenly needs:
- 🏠 Home Cleaning
- ❄ AC Repair Assistant
- 🍳 One-Day Cook
- 🎉 Function Helper
- 🍽 Catering Boys
- 🪑 Event Setup Workers
- 🚚 Load & Unload Helpers
- 🧱 Construction Helper
- 🚗 Driver for One Day
- 🚜 Farm Helper
- 📦 Shop Helper

Only include work that can realistically start within hours.

## 3. The Employer Experience (Post in 40 Seconds)

Current apps ask for Resumes, Experience, Education, Interviews, and make employers wait.

With **⚡ Instant Hire**, DutyPe asks:
- **"What work?"** (e.g., Cleaning, Cook, Helper, Driver, Painter)
- **"Where?"** (Current Location)
- **"How much?"** (e.g., ₹800)
- **"When?"** (Now, Today, Tomorrow)
- **Number of workers?** (1, 2, 5)
- **Notes** (Optional)

Done in 30–40 seconds. 

---

## 4. The Worker Experience

The flow is built for maximum speed and simplicity:

1. Worker opens DutyPe.
2. Turns ON **🟢 Available Now**.
3. A Nearby Instant Hire appears (e.g., *📍2.4 km away, Need 2 Catering Boys, ₹900, Starts in 30 mins*).
4. Worker taps **Apply**.
5. Employer calls.
6. Worker reaches location.
7. Work completed.
8. Employer marks completed & Payment is done.

### 🟢 Available Now Toggle
Workers can toggle between **🟢 Available Now** and **🔴 Busy**. Employers only see available workers, dramatically improving response times.

### Smart Notifications
Instead of spamming everyone, DutyPe notifies only workers who match:
- **Distance** (e.g., within 5km)
- **Skill**
- **Available Now** status

*Example notification: Driver Needed • 1.8 km away • ₹850 • Starts in 45 mins. This feels personal instead of spam.*

## 7. Monetization Strategies for Instant Hire

To maximize liquidity and user acquisition, we evaluated three monetization strategies.

### 🌟 Strategy 1: The "Hook" Strategy (100% Free / Limited Free) - *Currently Active*
**How it works:** 
Employers get **3 Free Instant Hires** (lifetime). Anyone can post an urgent need to experience the "magic" of DutyPe. After 3 free posts, they must use credits from their regular subscription.
**Why it’s the best:** 
Urgent work is an emergency. If you put a paywall in front of a panicked user, they leave. Give them a worker in 10 minutes for free, and they trust DutyPe forever. When they need a full-time worker, they will happily pay.

### 💰 Strategy 2: Pay-Per-Post Micro-transactions
**How it works:** 
Instead of an Instant Hire subscription, charge a tiny, flat fee per urgent post (e.g., ₹19 or ₹29).
**Why it works:** 
In an emergency, users do not want to evaluate complex subscription tiers (₹49 for 3 vs ₹99 for 5). A simple impulse buy of ₹19 via UPI removes the mental burden.

### 📦 Strategy 3: The "All-in-One" Unified Subscription
**How it works:** 
Keep only ONE subscription plan, but increase its value by bundling Instant Hires. 
*Example: ₹499/month includes 10 Regular Jobs + 5 Free Instant Jobs.*
**Why it works:** 
It makes the core subscription look incredibly valuable and keeps the pricing page clean (only one subscription to buy) while still monetizing the feature.
