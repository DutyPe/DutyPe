⚠️ Important Safety Rules

🚫 NEVER Pay for Jobs

Legitimate employers NEVER ask workers to pay money. If anyone asks you to pay registration fees, security deposits, or any other charges to get a job - it's a SCAM. Report immediately!

🔍 Verify Before You Go

  • Research the company online before visiting
  • Verify the job location on Google Maps
  • Check if the company has a website or social media
  • Look for reviews from other workers

📍 Share Your Location

  • Tell a family member or friend where you're going
  • Share the job details and employer contact
  • Set a check-in time with someone you trust
  • Use location sharing on your phone

🤝 Meet in Safe Places

  • First meetings should be in public places
  • Avoid isolated locations for interviews
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, leave
  • Don't go alone if you're uncomfortable

🚨 Red Flags to Watch For

Be cautious if:

  • Job pays unusually high for simple work
  • Employer asks for money upfront
  • Vague job description with no clear duties
  • Pressure to decide immediately
  • Requests for personal documents before hiring
  • Interview location is a private residence
  • Employer refuses to provide company details
  • Communication only through personal WhatsApp
