# Khammam launch plan (Oct 2026 →) — jobs, urgent work and home services

> One city, done properly. Win Khammam town first (≈ 3–5 km around Wyra Road, Gandhi Chowk,
> Mamillagudem, Khanapuram Haveli, the bus stand and the new collectorate), then the rest of the
> district. Everything below is built for tier-2 / tier-3 people who use WhatsApp and YouTube,
> trust faces more than ads, and read Telugu first.

## 1. What we sell, in one line each

| Product | For whom | One-line pitch (Telugu first) | How DutyPe earns |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jobs (vacancies)** | Shops, malls, hospitals, offices, homes ↔ local workers | "మీ దగ్గర్లోనే ఉద్యోగాలు. ఫీజు లేదు." | Employer plans after 3 free posts/day; referral free posts |
| **Urgent work** | Anyone who needs people *today* (loading, events, a day's help) | "ఇప్పుడే మనిషి కావాలా? నిమిషాల్లో." | 3 free urgent posts, then credits |
| **DutyPe Services** | Homes that need AC / cleaning / electrician / plumber / repair | "ఫిక్స్‌డ్ ధర. పని తర్వాత చెల్లింపు." | ₹19 booking fee (customer, collected by partner) + ₹19 partner fee per job, both from partner credits |

**Urgent vs Services — keep them separate in every message.** Urgent = *you* hire a worker for a
few hours and manage the work yourself (labour). Services = *DutyPe* sends a verified, skilled
partner for a fixed-price job at home, with a start code, rating and free re-work (a finished
service). Same people can earn on both, but customers should never be confused about which one
they are booking.

### Who is who (one person can be several of these)

| Name in the app | Who it is | What they do in DutyPe |
|---|---|---|
| **Worker** | Anyone looking for work | Applies to jobs, accepts urgent work |
| **Employer** | Shop, mall, office, hospital, home that needs staff | Posts jobs and urgent needs, hires |
| **Customer** | Anyone (employer *or* worker) who needs a repair/cleaning at home | Books DutyPe Services |
| **Service partner** | A worker DutyPe has approved for home services (cleaner, electrician, plumber, AC tech…) | Gets service bookings, does the job, keeps the money minus ₹19 |
| **DutyPe** | Us | Runs the app, verifies people, sets fair prices, takes a small fee |

A worker becomes a partner by tapping *Earn more with DutyPe Services* on the worker home and
passing the check. The same worker can still apply to normal jobs and urgent work (only one
unfinished job at a time).

### How money reaches DutyPe (no payment gateway needed)

Example: AC service ₹449, customer's first booking (booking fee ₹19 waived), partner's 2nd job.

1. Customer pays the partner **₹449** (cash/UPI) — the amount shown in the app.
2. The partner keeps prepaid **credits** with DutyPe (topped up by UPI to DutyPe's UPI ID,
   approved by the admin after checking the UTR).
3. When the partner taps *Complete*, the app deducts DutyPe's share from credits automatically:
   booking fee ₹19 − ₹19 first-booking offer + ₹19 partner fee = **₹19**.
4. Partner keeps ₹449 − ₹19 = **₹430**. DutyPe already has ₹19 in its bank from the top-up.

With a coupon the partner's earning never changes — the coupon comes out of DutyPe's fee, and it
is capped at DutyPe's fee so DutyPe never pays from its pocket. Credits are non-refundable and
stay in the wallet for future jobs.

## 2. Supply first (the rule every marketplace learns)

A customer who books and gets "No partner available" is lost for months. Before any customer
advertising for a service category, have **at least 8–10 approved, online partners** in that
category inside the launch zone (Urban Company's rule of thumb is ~20 per category per city; for a
tier-2 town 8–10 active ones is the minimum). Order:

1. **Weeks 1–3:** sign up partners (cleaning, electricians, plumbers, AC techs) and workers.
2. **Weeks 2–4:** sign up employers (shops, hospitals, schools, PG / hostels, builders).
3. **Week 4 onward:** customer marketing for the categories that have supply. Keep the rest
   hidden in the admin panel (switch the service off) until they do.

Where to find partners: electrical / hardware / sanitary shops (they know every electrician and
plumber — give the shop owner ₹50 per partner who completes 3 jobs), ITI Khammam passing-out
students, AC dealers' freelance technicians, mestri (labour contractor) networks, and the
morning labour adda near the bus stand.

## 3. Skilled vs basic work (who can do what)

* **Basic** (house sweeping/mopping, utensils, home help, shifting, car/bike wash): no training
  needed — anyone careful and honest. Approve after ID check + a polite call. These are the
  fastest way to give income to people without a trade.
* **Skilled ⚡** (electrical, AC, plumbing, appliances, carpentry, painting): mistakes cause fires,
  leaks, shocks. Approve a category only after a **skill check**:
  1. 3 practical questions on the phone (e.g. "MCB keeps tripping — what do you check first?").
  2. Photos / videos of past work, or the shop they worked at (call the shop), or ITI certificate.
  3. If still unsure, one **trial job at a team member's home** before going live.
  4. New skilled partners get simpler jobs (fan/switch/tap) for the first 5 jobs; full category
     after 5 jobs with ≥ 4.5★.
* A basic worker who wants to become skilled: pair them as a **helper** with a senior partner for
  2–4 weeks (DutyPe can add "helper" bookings later), then do the skill check.

The app now enforces this: skilled categories need ≥ 1 year experience and stay unticked in the
admin panel until you verify them.

## 4. Money: how we collect (and why not escrow yet)

**Current model (keep it):** the customer pays the partner directly (cash/UPI) after the work, the
amount shown in the app. The partner keeps a **prepaid credit wallet** with DutyPe (top-up by UPI,
verified by UTR). After each job DutyPe deducts its fee (booking fee + ₹19 partner fee; first job
free). For jobs / urgent work, money never passes through DutyPe.

Why this is right for Khammam now:

* Tier-2/3 customers trust "pay after the work, to the person who did it". Paying a startup
  first and waiting for the worker is a hard sell and needs refunds/support.
* **Collecting customers' money in our bank account and paying workers later makes DutyPe a
  payment aggregator / escrow under RBI rules** (Payment Aggregator directions). Doing it
  through a normal current account, with manual payouts from the admin panel, is legally risky,
  slow (every payout is manual), and creates disputes ("I paid, where is my money?").
* If we later want prepaid booking (online offers, corporate clients), do it with a licensed
  gateway's **marketplace/split product** (e.g. Razorpay Route, Cashfree Easy Split, PayU
  marketplace) — the gateway holds the money and pays the partner's bank/UPI; we only keep our
  share. Partners then add a UPI ID once, in their profile; customers never ask for it.
* Simple rule for tier-2 people: **"పని అయ్యాక, యాప్‌లో చూపిన డబ్బు, పని చేసిన వారికే."**

Keep the partner fee small (₹9–₹19) and flat. Do not take a % of the job in year one: partners
in small towns compare us with "no app at all", and a flat ₹19 is easy to understand.

## 6. The umbrella desk (your marketing-person idea — yes, do it)

It is the single best channel for tier-2/3: people trust a face, get help with OTP and the
app, and leave registered. See **growth/playbooks/umbrella_desk.md** for the full routine.
In short:

* Spots by time: **bus stand 7–10 am** (workers), **Wyra Road / main bazaar 11 am–2 pm**
  (shop owners), **hospital & college gates 3–6 pm**, **Sunday market**.
* The agent registers people on **dutype.in/join?agent=CODE** (works on any phone, no login),
  helps them install the app, and the system credits the agent automatically when that phone
  number signs up in the app. Pay the agent **per person who joins**, not per form, so fake
  entries earn nothing.
* Admin → *Field registrations* shows leads per agent per day, who joined, and lets the team
  mark people as called / onboarded.

## 7. Instagram and creators

* Pick 3–5 **Khammam-based** creators with real local followers (check comments are Telugu and
  local, not bought). 10k–50k followers with good engagement beat 500k city pages.
* Brief: a real story, not an ad — "AC stopped in the heat, booked on DutyPe, technician came in
  45 minutes, paid after". Give each creator **their own coupon code** (admin → Prices &
  settings → coupons) to measure bookings.
* Post formats from the admin Marketing kit: 4:5 post (1080×1350) and 9:16 story (1080×1920),
  text inside the safe zones.
* 2–3 posts a week from the DutyPe page: one partner story ("Ravi, electrician, earned ₹18,000
  this month"), one customer tip, one offer.

**Weekly review (every Friday):** bookings, no-partner rate, cancellations, ratings < 4, partner
earnings, cost per joined user per channel. Cut the worst channel, double the best.

## 9. Mistakes to avoid

* Advertising a service before there are partners for it.
* Cheap prices to win — partners leave, quality drops. Keep prices in the local middle
  (the catalog is set from Khammam/Hyderabad market rates) and win on reliability.
* Taking money from customers before we have a licensed split-payment setup.
* Paying field agents per form instead of per joined user.
* Letting a skilled category go live without a skill check.
* Too many offers at once: one festival offer at a time, capped at our own fee.

## 10. How we make money (simple unit economics, per completed booking)

* Booking fee ₹19 + partner fee ₹19 = **₹38 per booking** (₹0–19 on first bookings / first jobs).
* 30 bookings/day in month 3 ≈ ₹1,140/day ≈ **₹34k/month** from services, plus employer plans.
* Biggest costs: field agent, posters, SMS/WhatsApp OTP. Keep login on WhatsApp/Truecaller to cut
  SMS cost.
