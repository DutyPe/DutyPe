# SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM

## 🚨 The Problem We're Solving

### 📍 Location Mismatch
Workers waste hours traveling to jobs far from home. A cleaner won't travel 20km for a 2-hour shift.

### 🎭 Rampant Fraud
"Earn ₹50,000/day from home" scams flood job boards. Workers lose money to fake registration fees.

### 📱 Complex Interfaces
Existing apps are designed for IT professionals. Blue-collar workers struggle with complex forms.

### 💸 Payment Uncertainty
Workers fear not getting paid. Employers fear workers not showing up. No trust mechanism exists.

# SLIDE 3: OUR SOLUTION

## ✨ Our Solution: DutyPe

### 📍 Hyperlocal First

# SLIDE 5: HOW IT WORKS

## ⚙️ How DutyPe Works

**Step 1: Sign Up**
Quick OTP or Google login. Choose Worker or Employer role.

**Step 2: Complete Profile**
Simple profile: Photo, Skills, Location. No resume needed.

**Step 3: Discover Jobs**
See nearby jobs sorted by distance. Filter by category & pay.

**Step 4: 1-Tap Apply**
Apply instantly with auto-filled profile. Track status in real-time.

**Step 5: Get Hired!**
Receive notifications. Connect with employer. Start working.

# SLIDE 9: SAFETY & TRUST

## 🛡️ Safety & Trust Features

### 🔒 Developer Mode Detection
Detects fake GPS risk. Shows security warning.

### 📍 Location Verification
GPS-based verification. Flags suspicious postings.

### 👤 Age Validation
Users must be 18-70 years old.

### 📱 Device Fingerprinting
Prevents banned users from creating new accounts.

### ⭐ Two-Way Rating System
Workers rate employers, employers rate workers.

### 🚫 Dual-Role Prevention
Same phone cannot be both Worker and Employer.
