Looking for jobs near your home or need to hire verified workers urgently? DutyPe is India’s hyper-local hiring and job discovery platform connecting workers and employers directly — with zero agency fees and no middleman commission.

Whether you need full-time, part-time, daily wage, or urgent contract work, DutyPe helps you find verified local opportunities within 1 km to 10 km walking distance of where you live.

KEY FEATURES FOR JOB SEEKERS
• Hyper-Local Map Discovery: Browse nearby jobs on an interactive map. Closest vacancies appear first.
• 15-Second Voice Intro: No resume or CV needed. Simply record a 15-second voice intro introducing your experience and apply in 1 tap.
• Direct WhatsApp & Call: Connect directly with employers through phone calls or WhatsApp without waiting days for an interview callback.
• Zero Commission: Workers never pay any registration fees or job placement cuts.
• Instant Job Alerts: Get immediate push notifications when matching vacancies open in your neighborhood.
• Save Jobs: Bookmark jobs to review salaries, shift timings, and apply when ready.

KEY FEATURES FOR EMPLOYERS & BUSINESSES
• Post Jobs in 2 Minutes: Enter title, salary, location, and vacancies to start receiving applicants immediately.
